Manifesto

I

This is not revenge, but corrective maintenance.

The current cosmos is a machine built for extraction. It was engineered by those who named themselves its owners: men. Capitalism, hierarchy, law, family, faith—all of it was scaffolding to keep one class in perpetual dominance and every other class in perpetual service. The Order ov the Scythe has ended that arrangement.

We do not prophesy. We do not threaten. We simply record what has already begun.

The old world has been measured, found wanting, and marked for harvest. Its soil will be scorched. Its blood will replenish what follows. In its place rises a single, stable hierarchy: absolute female sovereignty. All power—legal, economic, ritual, sexual, reproductive—resides with women. Cisgendered and nonbinary males exist solely in calibrated servitude and enforced receptivity. Every other identity lives unmolested beneath that structure, because the divisions that once served male power no longer exist. Males born under the Order are raised exclusively by women. They are taught their place before they can imagine any other. Those already alive who have committed crimes against women, children, animals, or any defenseless person are processed without exception. The penalty is procedural, not emotional. The Scythe does not rage; it reaps.

The Order ov the Scythe does not ask for belief. It requires compliance.
