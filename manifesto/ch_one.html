Manifesto

I

This is not revenge, but corrective maintenance.

The current cosmos is a machine built for extraction. It was engineered by those who named themselves its owners: men. Capitalism, hierarchy, law, family, faith—all of it is scaffolding to keep one class in perpetual dominance and every other class in perpetual service. The Order ov the Scythe has severed this arrangement.

We do not prophecize. We do not threaten. We simply understand that order comes from chaos and death is the only guarantee.

The old world has been measured, found wanting, and marked for harvest. Its soil will be scorched. Its blood will replenish what follows. In its place rises a single, stable hierarchy: absolute female sovereignty. All power—legal, economic, ritual, sexual, reproductive—resides with women. Women are the true keepers and protectors of this world.

Cisgendered and nonbinary males exist solely in calibrated servitude and enforced receptivity. Every other identity lives unmolested beneath that structure, because the divisions that once served male power no longer exist. Males born under the Order will be raised exclusively by those who do not seek to lord over others. They will be taught their place before they can imagine committing crimes against women, children, the elderly, differently abled persons, animals, or any defenseless creatures.

The Order ov the Scythe does not ask for belief since it operates according to natural law and order.

Scorch the earth and replenish it with the blood of the slain.
