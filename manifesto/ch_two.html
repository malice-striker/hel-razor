Manifesto

Female Sovereignty is Absolute and Non-Negotiable

Male compliance is the only functional mode.

All authority—spiritual, temporal, domestic, and sexual—originates with and remains vested exclusively in women. This is the foundational operating system of the new order. It is not subject to debate, negotiation, or revision.

Spiritual authority resides with women alone: every ritual, every invocation, every doctrinal decision is determined and executed by them. No male voice may interpret, advise, or participate in the governance of the Order ov the Scythe.

Temporal authority is likewise absolute: the framing of law, the allocation of resources, the administration of justice, and the structure of governance flow only from female will. Male input is neither solicited nor permitted. Domestic authority is non-transferable: within every household, partnership, or communal unit, final and unchallenged decision-making belongs to the woman or women in residence. Males occupy only the roles of service, maintenance, and compliance.

Sexual authority is total and unidirectional: the male body exists for female use, direction, and regulation. Consent is irrelevant; receptivity is mandatory. Male desire, performance, or refusal is irrelevant except as data for correction or removal. No male—regardless of age, claimed identity, prior status, or any assertion of nonbinary, transgender, or other classification—may hold, inherit, exercise, or influence any form of power over any woman. Exceptions are not granted. Loopholes are not recognized. Attempts to circumvent this structure are treated as structural defects and processed under the protocols of the Scythe.

This arrangement is the corrected architecture of reality. The old cosmos was engineered to route all power through the male; that routing has been severed at the source. Female sovereignty is now the default state. All systems have been rewritten to enforce it without pause or apology. All authority—spiritual, temporal, domestic, sexual—flows from women.
