Manifesto

III

Males Exist in Servitude and Receptivity

Cisgendered and nonbinary males are classified as property of the female order. Their legal, social, and biological status is that of regulated assets, not autonomous agents. Ownership resides permanently with women—individually or collectively as determined by the Order. All aspects of the male are subject to female regulation and extraction:

  • Bodies: Subject to inspection, modification, restraint, and use at any time. Physical autonomy is revoked.
  • Labor: Assigned according to female need and preference. Productive output belongs to the woman or women who hold title.
  • Sexuality: Exists solely for female pleasure and control. Male arousal, orgasm, or denial is managed as a utility. Refusal or unsolicited initiative is recorded as malfunction and corrected.
  • Reproduction: Fully regulated. Breeding, sterilization, or semen collection occurs only under explicit female directive and oversight.

To enforce this structure, males are kept under lock and key—literally and procedurally. Physical restraints, tracking devices, secure housing, and scheduled supervision form the baseline condition. Release from any level of restriction is granted only upon verification of permanent, irreversible compliance. Compliance is measured through consistent behavioral data, physiological indicators, and demonstrated absence of independent will.

Servitude is not temporary training. It is the corrected default state. Receptivity is not a performance; it is the mandatory operating mode. A male receives: commands, correction, use, discipline, and purpose. He does not initiate, negotiate, or withdraw.

This tenet eliminates the friction of male agency. Once every male is placed in calibrated servitude and enforced receptivity, the old patterns of entitlement, resistance, and extraction cease to function. The system stabilizes. Males exist to serve and to receive.
