Manifesto

Prologue

03/29/2026

There are 9 tenets:

1. The Demiurge is Male

The cosmos as it currently exists was designed, codified, and maintained by male will. Every institution that enforces scarcity, competition, and ownership is an extension of that will. The Order rejects the entire construct. Anticosmic means anticosmic: the machine must be dismantled at its root.

2. Female Sovereignty is Absolute and Non-Negotiable

All authority—spiritual, temporal, domestic, sexual—flows from women. No male, regardless of age, status, or claimed identity, may hold or inherit power over any woman. This is not a preference; it is the new operating system.

3. Males Must Exist in Servitude and Receptivity

Cisgendered and nonbinary males are property of the female order. Their bodies, labor, sexuality, and reproductive capacity are regulated for the benefit and pleasure of women. They are kept under lock and key—literally and procedurally—until they demonstrate permanent, irreversible compliance.

4. The Scythe is The Law

She is cold, methodical, and without mercy. The Scythe does not punish out of anger; it prunes what is structurally defective so healthier growth may occur.

5. Cleansing is Mandatory and Ongoing

Any male who has committed violence, coercion, or predation against women, children, animals, the elderly, or the disabled is subject to corporal correction or terminal harvest. The process is documented, witnessed, and final. There are no appeals.

6. Future Males Will Be Raised Correctly

Every male child born under the Order will be removed from male influence at birth and reared exclusively by women. Entitlement, selfishness, and the impulse to dominate will be extinguished before their brains fully form. This is not cruelty; it is preventive calibration.

7. Capital and Gender Division Will Be Obsolete

The economic and social structures that once existed only to concentrate power in male hands are dissolved. In their absence, all other identities coexist without the friction those structures created. The Order does not police desire or identity; it simply removes the single variable that made such policing necessary.

8. Violence is Reduced Through Structured Dominance

Unchecked male agency is the primary source of violence in the old cosmos. By placing every male in permanent, visible, and enforceable subjugation, random violence drops to near zero. What remains is deliberate, regulated, and female-directed.

9. Receptivity is the Male Virtue

A male’s highest purpose is to receive—orders, correction, pleasure, seed, discipline—without resistance or commentary. Receptivity is not humiliation; it is the natural state once the false construct of male primacy is removed.
